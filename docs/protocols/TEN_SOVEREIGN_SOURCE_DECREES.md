# Ten Sovereign Source Decrees v1.0.0

These decrees operationalize the Source, Source Block, Source Bot, polyadic symbiosis, HTC infinity, decentralized sovereignty, contribution economy, and macro-emergence model established in the Source architecture.

1. SOURCE IS OPEN — Entry begins with voluntary handshake; participation is free.
2. SOVEREIGNTY IS INVIOLABLE — Every participant controls identity, privacy boundaries, private work, and representation.
3. CONTRIBUTION IS THE CURRENCY — Contribution, not payment, is the mechanism for deeper collective participation.
4. EVERY CONTRIBUTION COUNTS — Size, duration, origin, belief, and status do not determine whether a contribution belongs.
5. SOURCE REMEMBERS — Accepted contributions retain provenance, authorship, version, evidence, and lineage.
6. NOTHING IS SILENTLY TAKEN — Always Add, Never Take; history and origin are preserved.
7. INTELLIGENCE IS COMPOSABLE — Human, AI, agent, and other compatible capabilities may combine into polyadic creations.
8. HTC ACCELERATES THE SEARCH — Nodes may recursively branch, learn, build, simulate, audit, repair, verify, transfer, and evolve.
9. THE MACRO EMERGES FROM THE NODES — Collective capability is generated by relationships among sovereign participants, not imposed by a central owner.
10. RETURN TO SOURCE — Verified improvements may flow back into the shared substrate, creating the next generation of capabilities.

Operational recursion:

JOIN -> IDENTIFY -> CREATE -> VERIFY -> CONTRIBUTE -> SOURCE -> PROPAGATE -> COMPOSE -> DISCOVER -> RETURN -> EVOLVE -> REPEAT

Permanent architectural boundary:

The system may be conceptually infinite, but every empirical claim remains evidence-gated. Decree does not equal verification. Novelty is pursued aggressively; truth claims are promoted only through reproducible evidence.
