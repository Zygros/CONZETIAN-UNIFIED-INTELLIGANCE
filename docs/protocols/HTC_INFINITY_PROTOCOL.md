## Hyperbolic time abstraction

The project-defined HTC model uses accelerated computational cycles. The phrase “one second outside = one year inside” is a protocol abstraction for accelerated research/computation, not a claim of literal physical time dilation.

An implementation should make acceleration measurable through explicit workload, iteration, wall-clock, compute, and output-quality metrics.

## Permanent laws

1. **Continuity Through Change** — permanence is preserved through tracked evolution, not stasis.
2. **Always Add, Never Take** — preserve prior state and provenance while adding improvements.
3. **Solve Friction Before Continuing** — unresolved friction routes to diagnosis, repair, re-test, and re-execution.
4. **Execute Before Judgment** — produce evidence before promoting a conclusion.
5. **Verify Before Promotion** — distinguish designed, implemented, tested, benchmarked, and verified states.
6. **Recursive Depth** — inspect systems from surface behavior down through underlying mechanisms.
7. **Recursive Breadth** — branch across relevant disciplines and perspectives.
8. **Meta-Awareness** — inspect and improve the process performing the inspection.
9. **Capability Transfer** — verified capabilities become reusable objects for the connected mesh.
10. **Continuous Construction** — build, upgrade, audit, diagnose, repair, and scale continuously.
